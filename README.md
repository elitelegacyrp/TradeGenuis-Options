## 🎯 What Is TradeGenuis-Options?

TradeGenuis-Options is a powerful desktop research workstation for trading US stocks and Binance crypto options. It combines real-time market data, smart signal scanning, and AI-powered analysis to help you make better trading decisions.

Think of it as your personal trading analyst that works 24/7, scanning the markets for opportunities, creating detailed research reports, and tracking your trading performance - all in one place.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔍 Opportunity Scanner
- Scans your watchlist for **7 types of trading signals**:
  - Volatility premium
  - Event premium
  - Cheap volatility
  - Momentum signals
  - Skew extremes
  - Term inversions
  - 0DTE gamma plays
- Each signal includes a concrete trade structure with real options chain data
- Trades show legs, premium, win rate, risk/reward ratio, and expected value
- **Smart filtering**: Only shows trades with positive expected value
- Every signal includes data source and timestamp

### 📊 Research Reports
- Get real-time options chain data (ATM IV, straddles, expected volatility, P/C ratio, 25Δ skew, max open interest)
- AI-powered research reports generated by DeepSeek in five structured sections:
  1. **Conclusion**
  2. **Evidence**
  3. **Strategy**
  4. **Risks**
  5. **Confidence Level**
- Reports are automatically saved to your knowledge base
- Ask follow-up questions or adopt a report as your trading plan with one click

### 📝 Trading Journal
- **AI Plan Checker**: Before entering a trade, AI reviews it against your trading rules, frameworks, and history
- Grades: Pass / Pass with modifications / Don't trade
- Automatically logs positions and settlements
- Records lessons learned for future reference
- **Performance Dashboard**:
  - Brier scores for forecast accuracy
  - Confidence calibration charts
  - Setup-based expected value statistics

### 📚 Knowledge Base
- Store and manage:
  - Trading frameworks
  - Strategy templates
  - Research reports
  - Trading lessons
  - Trader profiles
- Everything is editable Markdown
- Import from MD, TXT, or PDF files

### 💬 Smart Q&A
- Ask any question about markets or trading
- System searches both your knowledge base and real-time data
- AI generates concise answers with evidence first
- Every answer cites its source pages
